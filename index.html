Village Pt

Aspen, Colorado

Nestled amid tranquil pine forests, Village Pt exemplifies DWEL's core philosophy of quiet luxury and spatial harmony. The cantilevered structure frames panoramic valley vistas while sustainable cedar timber and obsidian zinc cladding age gracefully alongside the surrounding ecosystem.

Area 480 m²
Lead Architect Elena Rostova
Materials Zinc, Timber, Glass
Status Completed

DWEL® Spatial Vision

A meditative study of timber, light, and landscape architecture.

DWEL® STUDIO

ABOUT DWEL

DWEL® is an international architecture and spatial design practice founded in 2018. We operate at the intersection of minimalist aesthetics, human comfort, and environmental mindfulness.

Every commission is approached as a bespoke dialogue between the client's aspirations and the raw genius loci of the site.

DESIGN VALUES

  • Clarity: Distilling complexity into pure, expressive architectural forms.
  • Nature Integration: Blurring boundaries between interior sanctuaries and natural landscapes.
  • Permanence: Selecting authentic materials that patinate beautifully over decades.
